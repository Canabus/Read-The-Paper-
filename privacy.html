Privacy Policy — Read the Paper

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This is the privacy policy for Read the Paper, in plain English — same as everything else around here. The short version: the app runs on your device, your papers stay on your device, and there is nothing collecting anything about you. The long version is below, and it's not much longer.

The whole policy in one line: No account. No sign-up. No uploads. No analytics. No ads. No tracking. No selling or sharing your data — because the app never gets your data in the first place.

How the app works

Read the Paper runs entirely on your device, in your browser. When you open a document or a photo of a document in the app, it is processed right there on your device. Nothing you open is uploaded to a server — there is no server for it to go to. That's why the app works in airplane mode, and you can test that yourself: load it once, turn airplane mode on, and run a paper through it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, or store on any server:

  • The documents, bills, contracts, or photos you open in the app
  • Your name, email address, or any account information — there are no accounts
  • Your location
  • Usage data, analytics, or device identifiers

What stays on your device

Some things you do inside the app — for example, entries you type into the bills tracker — are saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device, so they're still there when you come back. That storage never leaves your device and is never sent anywhere. The app includes a wipe / clear control that deletes everything the app has saved locally. Clearing your browser's site data also removes it.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Read the Paper has no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. There are no ad networks, no trackers, and no third-party services watching what you do in the app.

Sharing your information

We do not share your personal information with third parties, because we do not receive your personal information. There is nothing to share, sell, or hand over.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If how the app handles information ever changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it. The version posted here is the one that applies.
