See it work first

Here's what it does

This is the sample bill built right into the app — City Power & Water, customer "J. Sample," 12 Example Street. Made up on purpose. Your paper never has to touch a thing for you to see what the app does with a clause like this one:

What the paper says
"If payment is received after the Due Date, a late fee of $15 and interest of 1.5% per month on the unpaid balance will apply."

Off the sample bill — total due $186.42, due date December 2, 2026.

In plain English

Pay late and two things hit you: a flat $15 fee, right away — and then 1.5% interest every month on whatever you still owe. That interest works out to about 18% a year, and it keeps ticking until the bill is paid off.

What to watch

The whole clause hangs on one date — December 2. The stub at the bottom says it flat out: "The stub alone is not payment," so mailing the coupon without the money doesn't count. And the top half tells you to keep it date-stamped when you pay, because that's your proof. The paper tells on itself — if you read it.
